Katsina APC Consensus Update

Agreement Reached on Senatorial Candidate for Funtua Zone

Following a meeting held with key stakeholders from the Funtua Zone, alongside the Executive Governor of Katsina State, Malam Dikko Umaru Radda, Senator Muntari Dandutse has emerged as the consensus senatorial candidate for the zone.

Meanwhile, Hon. Hamza Sule Faskari has secured the party’s ticket to contest for the House of Representatives seat representing the Faskari/Sabuwa/Kankara Federal Constituency.

This resolution reflects the strong unity among party leaders and stakeholders, aimed at fostering reconciliation, strengthening cohesion, and positioning the party for victory in the 2027 general elections.
