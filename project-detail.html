About This Project

The Bakori Earth Dam Phase 2 is a major federal infrastructure project facilitated by Senator Dandutse Muntari Mohammed to address water scarcity and support agricultural productivity across Bakori LGA.

The dam will serve thousands of farming households, providing year-round irrigation capacity and improving food security across the Funtua Senatorial Zone.

Capacity 5M Litres
Beneficiaries 12,000+
LGA Coverage Bakori
